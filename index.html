How it works

Three steps. One of them is yours.

Nodpay sits between the software that knows who’s owed what, and the bank that holds the money. It never touches either’s job — it just removes the faff in the middle.

1

Xero does the typing

Your bookkeeper pulls the latest pay run — names, amounts, references — with one click. Nothing re-keyed, nothing pasted into a CSV, nothing to get wrong.

2

You give the nod

A notification lands on your phone. Total, headcount, anything unusual — then one tap, confirmed with Face ID in your own banking app. Thirty seconds, from anywhere.

3

Everyone’s paid in seconds

Faster Payments go straight from your account to your team’s — Nodpay never holds a penny. Watch each salary tick off as it lands.

Built paranoid

Anything unusual is impossible to miss.

Invoice fraud lives in the small print — a changed sort code, a payee you’ve never paid. Nodpay puts the small print in lights, before the button, every time. Payments only ever go to payees you’ve approved before, and every nod is logged: who prepared it, who approved it, and when.

Pricing

Pence per payment. Nothing per month.
