Questions

Asked and answered.

Why can't my bookkeeper make payments from our Starling business account?

Starling only gives payment access to directors and PSCs — there's no bookkeeper or delegate role. Nodpay fixes it without changing banks: your bookkeeper preps and checks the pay run in Nodpay, and you approve the whole thing with one tap, secured by Starling's own authorisation.

How does Nodpay work with Xero and Starling?

Nodpay pulls the posted pay run from Xero Payroll, matches every employee to your saved Starling payees, flags anything new or changed, and shows each person's difference versus last run. Your finance person signs it off with a payment date — and on that date, the nod comes to you.

Does Nodpay hold our money?

Never. Your money stays in your bank until the moment you nod, then goes directly from your account to your team's. Nodpay is not a wallet. Payments are initiated over Open Banking by an FCA-authorised payment initiation provider.

How much does it cost?

£2 per pay run + 20p per employee. No subscription, no tiers, no contract. Twelve people is about £4.40 a payday; thirty is £8.

Does the boss need an app?

No app, no password. The approval arrives as a secure link — review, nod, done. Your bank's own authorisation is the security step.
